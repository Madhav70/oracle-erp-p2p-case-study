> **Simulated Project Disclaimer**:

---

## 1. Communications Strategy & Core Principles

The communication strategy establishes five operating tenets:
1. **Transparency**: Regular reporting of milestones, risks, and challenges using objective RAG (Red/Amber/Green) criteria.
2. **Role Relevance**: Tailoring messages to the specific daily operational needs of each stakeholder group (avoiding technical jargon for plant floor staff).
3. **Multi-Channel Engagement**: Blending executive memorandums, team standups, hands-on workshops, intranet bulletins, and supplier letters.
4. **Bi-Directional Feedback**: Establishing continuous input mechanisms (pulse surveys, floor super-users, Q&A forums) to detect and resolve friction early.
5. **Cadence Consistency**: Adhering strictly to established meeting frequencies and reporting deadlines.

## 3. Feedback Loop & Pulse Survey Architecture

To ensure issues do not fester unaddressed, the PMO deploys three structured feedback loops:

```
[Level 1: Floor Super-Users]     -> Daily operational feedback logged at Dock and Plant workstations.
[Level 2: Weekly SME Roundtable] -> 30-minute Friday pulse check with AP and Purchasing leads.
[Level 3: Digital Pulse Surveys] -> Quantified 1-5 satisfaction scoring across Usability, Speed, and Support.
```

- **Trigger for Action**: If average user satisfaction drops below 3.5 / 5.0 in any department, the Change Lead initiates a targeted on-site coaching intervention within 48 hours.
